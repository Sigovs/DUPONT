CUT-OUTS (TEMPORARY: Alex's professional cut-outs replace them)
- Method: build/cutout.py. Local and deterministic (numpy/scipy/Pillow).
  - Cyc model plus colour-distance flood fill, seeded at the border.
  - Ring-line removal.
  - Scripted pockets and edge cleanup, standing in for GIMP.
  - 1 px choke with ≤1 px anti-aliasing, no blur or feather. Interior pixels are untouched.
  - Multiply ground plate: levels set so the darkest border floor value maps to 255, body painted white.
- Porsche (new): fuzz 22 / floor 70, flank scans y 600–1000.
  - The cut line follows the bumper and splitter (src y 1018 at the sides, 1052 at the centre), so the
    under-bumper shadow lives on the plate.
  - Plate levels v = 230. Plate border pixels ≠ 255: 0.
  - Known residue: two tiny grey tags at the splitter ends (temporary asset, accepted).
- Measured body bboxes (src px): Ford 389–1543 × 300–962 (1154 × 662), anchor (966, 962);
  Ferrari 390–1530 × 302–992 (1140 × 690), anchor (960, 992); Porsche 453–1472 × 349–1052 (1019 × 703),
  anchor (962.5, 1052). The AD's Porsche figures were 1015 wide with the anchor at y 1060.
