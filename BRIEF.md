## Agent responsibilities (summary)
- brand-extractor: identity, logos, type, colour tokens, conventions, voice, photo sources, verified facts, provenance.
  Separate verified / client-provided / creative interpretation / unverified. Other duPont divisions' identity is not automatically binding on Select.
- art-director: no code. Design Read, positioning, visual language, concept, hierarchy, composition rules, shot list,
  image needs, conceptual motion, one signature move, pacing. Avoid generic dealer layouts, random full-width car backgrounds,
  repetitive card grids, decorative type, gratuitous 3D, AI gradients, cheap scroll effects.
- designer: executes the approved concept; homepage, SRP preview, nav + dropdowns, working locations, search/filter sim,
  responsive, galleries, meaningful CTAs, footer. No dead buttons. Optimised images, no CLS, reduced motion.
- motion-designer: after structure + motion direction approved. Directed, not merely animated.
- design-critic: reports only; screenshots desktop/tablet/mobile; prioritised findings; re-critique after fixes; anti-patterns gate.
- project-recorder: at close only.
